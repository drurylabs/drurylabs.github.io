loom: privacy policy

Last change: 1 November 2026.

loom is a personal tool. Only its owner uses it, on the owner's own computer, with the owner's own Gmail accounts.

The data that loom reads

With the permission of the account, loom reads the Gmail filters, the labels, and the headers of messages (sender, subject and list headers). loom uses the headers only to count senders, so that its owner can decide which filters to make.

Where the data goes

The data goes from Google to the owner's computer, and to no other place. loom has no server. It sends no data to any other party, and it does not use the data for advertising or to train models. The OAuth tokens are in the macOS Keychain on the owner's computer.

Limited Use

loom's use and transfer of information that it receives from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How to remove the access

To remove loom's access to an account, open myaccount.google.com/permissions and remove loom. loom also deletes its token when its owner runs loom gmail logout.
